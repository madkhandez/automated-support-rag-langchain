ACME Corporation — Employee Leave & Time-Off Policy
=====================================================
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Policy Number: HR-2026-004
Department: Human Resources

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy establishes guidelines for all types of employee leave at ACME Corporation.
It applies to all full-time and part-time employees who have completed their probationary
period of 90 days. The policy aims to support work-life balance while maintaining
operational efficiency across all departments.

2. ANNUAL LEAVE (VACATION)
--------------------------
Full-time employees are entitled to 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive prorated leave based on their contracted hours. Annual leave
accrues at a rate of 1.25 days per month and may be carried over up to a maximum of
5 days into the following year. Unused leave beyond the carryover limit will be forfeited
on December 31st. Employees must submit leave requests at least 14 days in advance
through the HR portal. Managers must approve or deny requests within 3 business days.

3. SICK LEAVE
-------------
All employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not
accrue and resets on January 1st each year. For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days,
employees must provide a medical certificate from a licensed healthcare provider.
Employees should notify their immediate supervisor within 2 hours of their scheduled
start time when taking sick leave. Chronic illness accommodations are handled separately
under the Disability Accommodations Policy (HR-2026-007).

4. PARENTAL LEAVE
-----------------
ACME Corporation provides 16 weeks of paid parental leave for primary caregivers and
6 weeks for secondary caregivers. This applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement.
Parental leave must commence within 12 months of the qualifying event. Employees may
request a flexible return-to-work arrangement during the first 4 weeks after returning.

5. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
--------------------
Employees are granted up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an
immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Up to 3 days are granted for
extended family members (grandparent, in-law, aunt, uncle). Additional unpaid leave
may be requested through the HR department.

6. REMOTE WORK POLICY
---------------------
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Remote work
arrangements must be documented in the HR system. Employees are expected to maintain
regular working hours and be available for virtual meetings during core hours (10 AM
to 3 PM local time). Equipment and ergonomic assessments are available for home offices.

7. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT
-----------------------------
Upon termination, employees will be compensated for any unused accrued annual leave at
their current daily rate. Employees who resign must provide at least 2 weeks notice.
To discontinue service, employees should submit a formal resignation letter to their
manager and HR. The company reserves the right to terminate employment with cause as
outlined in the Employee Handbook section 12.

8. POLICY AMENDMENTS
--------------------
This policy is reviewed annually. Amendments require approval from the VP of Human
Resources and the CEO. Employees will be notified of changes at least 30 days in
advance via company email and the HR portal.
